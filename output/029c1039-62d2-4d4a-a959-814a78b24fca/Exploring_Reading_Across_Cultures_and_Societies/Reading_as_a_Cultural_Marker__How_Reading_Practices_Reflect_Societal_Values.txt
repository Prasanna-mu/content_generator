#### 4. Reading for Cultural Transmission

Reading can be a crucial component of cultural transmission, helping to preserve and disseminate cultural knowledge, traditions, and histories. This aspect of reading is often seen in literature, folklore, and cultural texts such as historical texts, literature, and religious texts.

**Example: Analyzing Cultural Texts**

```python
# Sample text from a cultural text
text = """
The Book of the Dead is an ancient Egyptian text.
It consists of spells and rituals intended to help the deceased in their journey to the afterlife.
"""

# Function to analyze the text for cultural references
def analyze_text(text):
    cultural_references = []
    for word in text.split():
        if word.lower() in ["afterlife", "journey"]:
            cultural_references.append(word)
    return cultural_references

# Example usage
cultural_references = analyze_text(text)
print(cultural_references)
```

**Explanation**: This function identifies specific cultural references within a text, such as terms related to the afterlife and journey. This analysis can reveal how texts function as cultural markers and how they shape societal beliefs and values.

#### 5. Reading as a Tool for Identifying and Challenging Societal Norms and Values

Reading can be an effective tool for challenging societal norms and values. By critically analyzing texts, readers can identify and question social constructs and biases. This process is often seen in works of literature that explore themes of social critique, identity, and power dynamics.

**Example: Critical Reading Techniques**

```python
def critical_reading(text):
    critical_thoughts = []
    for sentence in text.split('. '):
        if "power" in sentence.lower():
            critical_thoughts.append(sentence)
        if "justice" in sentence.lower():
            critical_thoughts.append(sentence)
    return critical_thoughts

# Example usage
text = """
The power of the rich often leads to injustice.
Social justice movements fight for the rights of the marginalized.
"""
critical_thoughts = critical_reading(text)
print("Critical Thoughts:", critical_thoughts)
```

**Explanation**: This function identifies sentences within a text that touch on themes of power and justice. This exercise can help readers identify potential biases and societal norms within a text.
