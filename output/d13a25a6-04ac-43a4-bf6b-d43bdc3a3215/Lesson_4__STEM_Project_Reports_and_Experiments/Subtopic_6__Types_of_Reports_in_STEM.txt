### Lesson: Writing Project Reports and Experiments

#### Subtopic: Types of Reports in STEM

In STEM fields, project reports come in various forms such as research reports, case studies, and technical documents. Understanding these types is crucial for effective report writing.

**Research Reports:**
Research reports detail the findings from a specific study or experiment. They often include hypotheses, methods, results, and conclusions. Example:
```plaintext
Research Report: The Effects of Different Salts on Plant Growth
- Hypotheses: Salt concentration affects plant growth.
- Methods: Seeds were planted in soil with varying salt concentrations.
- Results: Higher salt concentrations led to reduced plant growth.
- Conclusions: Salt affects plant growth, with negative impacts at higher concentrations.
```

**Case Studies:**
Case studies present a real-world problem and its solution. They are useful for understanding practical applications. Example:
```plaintext
Case Study: Implementing AI in Healthcare
- Problem: High costs and inefficiencies in healthcare diagnostics.
- Solution: AI algorithms for automated diagnostics.
- Results: Reduced costs and improved patient outcomes.
- Conclusion: AI shows promise in healthcare diagnostics.
```

**Technical Reports:**
These focus on the technical aspects of a project. They are often used to document findings and provide technical details. Example:
```plaintext
Technical Report: Developing a New Software Tool
- Overview: Tool for automating data entry in databases.
- Design: User-friendly interface, efficient algorithms.
- Implementation: Code written in Java, tested on different databases.
- Evaluation: User feedback, performance metrics.
```

Understanding these types helps in selecting the right format for each project, ensuring the report is clear, relevant, and effective in the STEM field.
