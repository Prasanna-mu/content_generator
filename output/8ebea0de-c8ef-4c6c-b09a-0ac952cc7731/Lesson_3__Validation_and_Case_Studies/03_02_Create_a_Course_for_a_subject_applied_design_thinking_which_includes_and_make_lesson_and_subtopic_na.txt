### Subtopic: User-Centric Innovation

#### Lesson: Validation and Case Studies

**Subtopic Description:**
Learn about problem and customer validation, user personas, and the customer development process. Understand how to validate a problem's significance and identify potential customers through interviews and field visits.

**Key Concepts:**
- Problem Validation: Identify and validate the problem to be solved.
- Customer Validation: Identify potential customers and validate their needs.
- User Persona: Create detailed profiles of your target users.
- Customer Development Process: Conduct interviews and field visits to understand users better.

**Activity: Customer Development Process**

**Steps:**
1. **Define the Problem:** Clearly define the problem you are trying to solve.
2. **Identify Target Users:** Define the user personas based on demographics, behaviors, and needs.
3. **Conduct Interviews:** Gather insights from users through structured interviews.
4. **Field Visits:** Observe users in their natural environment to understand their needs and challenges.
5. **Synthesize Findings:** Use insights from interviews and field visits to refine your understanding of the problem and potential users.

**Activity: Minimum Usable Prototype (MUP) Design**

**Steps:**
1. **Define the Challenge:** Clearly define the problem to be solved.
2. **Design a Concept:** Conceptualize the solution concept.
3. **Prototype:** Build a minimum viable prototype.
4. **Test:** Test the prototype with real users to gather feedback.
