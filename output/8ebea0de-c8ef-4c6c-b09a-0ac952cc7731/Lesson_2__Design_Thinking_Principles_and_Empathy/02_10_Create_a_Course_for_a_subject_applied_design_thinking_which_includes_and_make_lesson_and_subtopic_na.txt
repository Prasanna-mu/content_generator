### Subtopic 10: Concept Generation and Solution Exploration

#### Lesson: DESIGN THINKING 9 - Human-Centered Design

- **Understanding the Innovation Process**: Dive into the stages of human-centered design, including problem identification, validation, and solution exploration.
- **Empathy Building Techniques**: Learn advanced techniques to build empathy with users through interviews, observations, and user personas.
- **Risk Mitigation with FIR**: Explore the Forge 231 Ovation Rubric to mitigate validation risk early in the design process.
- **Case Studies**: Analyze real-world examples to understand how to validate opportunities and solve problems effectively.

### Activity: Customer Development Process
- **Customer Interviews & Field Visits**: Conduct structured interviews and field visits to gather insights from users, creating user personas and stories.
- **Customer Validation**: Validate your solution’s relevance and feasibility with real users, identifying pain points and opportunities.

### Concept of Minimum Usable Prototype (MUP)
- **MUP Challenge Brief**: Define a clear challenge and objectives for your MUP.
- **Designing & Crafting the Value Proposition**: Create a compelling value proposition to guide your design process.
- **Designing & Testing Value Proposition**: Test and refine your value proposition to ensure it resonates with your target users.

### Concept Generation and MUP Design
- **Conceptualize the Solution Concept**: Brainstorm and ideate potential solutions, focusing on user needs and market opportunities.
- **Explore, Iterate, and Learn**: Iteratively refine your concepts based on feedback and user testing.
- **Build the Right Prototype**: Develop a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) that demonstrates core functionality and value.

### Systematic Concept Generation
- **Evaluate Technology Alternatives**: Consider and evaluate different technology solutions that align with your concept.
- **Evaluate Solution Concepts**: Assess the feasibility and viability of your design concepts, ensuring they meet user needs and market demands.

### Subtopic Description: 90 Characters
Introduces concept generation and solution exploration techniques, including empathy-building, risk mitigation, and MUP design.
